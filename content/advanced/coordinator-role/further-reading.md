Optional. This Stage is complete without these.

**The reference file coordinators actually use.**
SATCS, *The Organ and Tissue Donation Reference File* (the Red File), available through the [Southern African Transplantation Society](https://sats.org.za/). Its sections on when to refer, legal requirements and family consent are the practical backbone of this Stage and the next.

**How one province has written the role down.**
Western Cape Department of Health and Wellness, *Organ and Tissue Donation Policy in the Western Cape*, Circular H 84/2025 (June 2025). The clearest South African statement of the triggers, the timing rule, the authorisations, theatre responsibilities and the audit loop. It is provincial policy, so other provinces may differ in detail, but it describes the same national role.

**The pathway vocabulary.**
Domínguez-Gil B, Delmonico FL, Shaheen FA, et al., *"The critical pathway for deceased donation: reportable uniformity in the approach to deceased donation"*, Transplant International 2011;24(4):373–378. The international source of the possible, potential, eligible, actual and utilised donor terms.

**📌 The Australian comparison.**
Organ and Tissue Authority, *Best Practice Guideline for Offering Organ and Tissue Donation in Australia* (2021). Useful for seeing how another country structures the same role and measures its effect. Not South African practice.

**If you want to go further.**
Thomson D, *Organ donation: From death to life*, a free course on [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/learn/organ-donation), recommended in the Red File's own reading list and taught by a South African critical care specialist.
