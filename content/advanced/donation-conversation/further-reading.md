Optional. This Stage is complete without these.

**FACTS, in full.**
Wilmans C, de Jager M, *Wits Transplant Procurement Handbook: A Practical Guide to Organ Donor Procurement*, 1st edition (Wits Transplant, September 2019). The source of FACTS, including the worked examples of what to say and what not to say. The original download link no longer works, but the FACTS section is reproduced in the SATCS Red File, available through the [Southern African Transplantation Society](https://sats.org.za/).

**Whether FACTS works.**
de Jager M, Wilmans C, Fabian J, Botha JF, Etheredge HR, *"Increasing deceased organ donor numbers in Johannesburg, South Africa: 18-month results of the Wits Transplant Procurement Model"*, South African Medical Journal 2019;109(9):626–631. The team that built FACTS reports one coordinator's consent rate before and after it. Read the tables as well as the abstract: the headline figures need their denominators beside them.

**📌 The evidence on decision time.**
Han SY, Kim JI, Lee EW, et al., *"Factors associated with a family's delay of decision for organ donation after brain death"*, Annals of Transplantation 2017;22:17–23. Short and readable. Read the discussion as well as the headline, because the authors are careful about what their numbers do and don't show.

**📌 The international version of the same arc.**
Organ and Tissue Authority, *Best Practice Guideline for Offering Organ and Tissue Donation in Australia* (2021). The five elements and their planning and review templates. Australian practice, not South African.

**The common ancestor.**
NHS Blood and Transplant, *Approaching the Families of Potential Organ Donors: Best Practice Guidance*. FACTS was built on its 2015 edition, and the Western Cape policy's family-approach appendix cites the 2013 edition.
