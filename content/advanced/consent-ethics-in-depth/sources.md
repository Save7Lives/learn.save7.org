- National Health Act 61 of 2003, sections 6 to 8, 62 and 65, on [gov.za](https://www.gov.za/documents/national-health-act)
- *Clarke v Hurst* (1992)
- Health Professions Council of South Africa, *Seeking Patients' Informed Consent: The Ethical Considerations* (Booklet 4, revised December 2021), sections 8 and 9, from the HPCSA's [ethical guidelines page](https://www.hpcsa.co.za/ethics)
- Health Professions Council of South Africa, *Guidelines for the Withholding and Withdrawing of Treatment* (Booklet 7, revised September 2025)
- Health Professions Council of South Africa, *Ethical Guidelines on Palliative Care* (Booklet 17, 2019)
- South African Law Commission, *Euthanasia and the Artificial Preservation of Life*, Project 86 report (November 1998), on the [Commission's site](https://www.justice.gov.za/salrc/)
- Western Cape Department of Health and Wellness, *Organ and Tissue Donation Policy in the Western Cape*, Circular H 84/2025 (June 2025)
- McQuoid-Mason D, *Human tissue and organ transplant provisions: Chapter 8 of the National Health Act and its Regulations, in effect from March 2012: what doctors must know*, South African Medical Journal 2012;102(9):733–735
- South African Transplant Coordinators Society (SATCS), *The Organ and Tissue Donation Reference File* (the Red File), available through the [Southern African Transplantation Society](https://sats.org.za/)
- Wilmans C, de Jager M, *Wits Transplant Procurement Handbook: A Practical Guide to Organ Donor Procurement*, 1st edition (Wits Transplant, September 2019)
