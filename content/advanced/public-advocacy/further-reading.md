Optional. This Stage is complete without these.

**ULUNTU, in the ODF's own words.**
The Organ Donor Foundation's [projects page](https://odf.org.za/projects/) describes the ULUNTU Awareness Campaign, alongside its work on a donor-register tool for transplant coordinators. Read it with this Stage's caution: it is a description of intent, not an evaluation.

**The ODF's awareness work and its limits.**
The SATCS Red File's section on the Organ Donor Foundation sets out the ODF's public awareness, education and donor-register work, including the Uluntu Project. It is also clear about what the ODF is not: it is not a medical organisation and has no role in procuring or allocating organs.

**The confidentiality rule, in the original.**
*Regulations regarding the general control of human bodies, tissue, blood, blood products and gametes*, GN R180, Government Gazette 35099 of 2 March 2012, regulation 24, on [gov.za](https://www.gov.za/). Short, and worth reading exactly, because its wording ("any fact", "may possibly be established") is broader than most people assume.

**How one province handles equity and the media.**
Western Cape Department of Health and Wellness, *Organ and Tissue Donation Policy in the Western Cape*, Circular H 84/2025 (June 2025), sections 18 (*Equality and diversity*) and 19 (*Media*). Two short sections that most of this Stage rests on.

**Where Save7 fits.**
Save7 is named after the same figure the ODF uses: one donor can save seven lives. This Course is Save7's own contribution to informed decision-making, and the standard in this Stage applies to it too.
