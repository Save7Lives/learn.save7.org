- **Withdrawing treatment is its own decision, made first.** HPCSA Booklet 7 (revised September 2025) holds that there is no duty to prolong life at all costs, that the decision is made in the patient's best interests with the family consulted, and that intending to end a life is unethical and unlawful.

- **In DCD, donation is considered only after an independent decision to withdraw treatment.** Nothing is done to hasten death, and if the heart does not stop in time, palliative care simply continues.

- **Accommodation gives a family time, with limits.** A brief, finite period of support after brain death, with the timeframe explained in advance, ordinarily no more than a day.

- **Families may seek a second opinion.** The determination-of-death guidelines allow a second clinician's opinion on brain death, and Booklet 7 gives patients and families the right to seek one. Serious disagreement goes to independent review.

- **Dignity is part of the care.** Calm, honest, compassionate conversations, respect for cultural practices, and a good death that donation never replaces.

- **Opt-out is not a shortcut.** 📌 International evidence finds that the law alone does not explain differences between countries, and switching has not reliably raised donation. South Africa's transplant community sees no evidence to presume that most South Africans would consent.

- **The evidence points at the chain:** referral, trained coordinators, family consent and public understanding, whatever the consent model.

- **Scarcity is real and unevenly shared.** 2,586 people were waiting at the end of 2021, 229 transplants were done and 189 people died waiting. Access is concentrated in cities and in private care.

- **Fairness applies to who is asked as well as who receives.** No family should be skipped on assumptions about background, and care must not be prejudiced by race, culture, status or belief.

- **Less-than-ideal donors can still save lives.** Donors with conditions such as hypertension, diabetes or HIV are assessed organ by organ, and donation is meant to be financially neutral.
