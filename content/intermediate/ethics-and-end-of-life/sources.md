- Republic of South Africa, *National Health Act 61 of 2003*, section 60.
- Department of Health, draft *Regulations on Organ Transplantation*, Government Notice 7879, 4 September 2026 (published for comment, not in force).
- Health Professions Council of South Africa, *Guidelines for the Withholding and Withdrawing of Treatment*, Booklet 7, revised September 2025, from the HPCSA's [ethical guidelines](https://www.hpcsa.co.za/).
- Thomson D, et al., [*South African guidelines on the determination of death*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10193841/), *Southern African Journal of Critical Care* 2021;37(1b):41-54.
- Western Cape Government Health, circular H84/2025 (the Western Cape donation policy).
- SATS and SATCS, report to the World Health Organization's Global Observatory, figures for 2021.
- Southern African Transplantation Society, *Organ and Tissue Donation in South Africa: Creating a National Strategy Roadmap*.
- South African Transplant Coordinators Society, *Organ and Tissue Donation Reference File* (the Red File).
- Etheredge HR, "Assessing Global Organ Donation Policies: Opt-In vs Opt-Out", *Risk Management and Healthcare Policy* 2021;14:1985-1998.
- de Jager et al., the Wits transplant procurement model, *SAMJ* 2019.
- Rithalia A, et al., [Impact of presumed consent for organ donation on donation rates: a systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2628300/), *BMJ* 2009.
- Shepherd et al., study of 48 countries, *BMC Medicine* 2014.
- Arshad et al., study of 35 OECD countries, *Kidney International* 2019.
- Noyes J, et al., [Short-term impact of introducing a soft opt-out organ donation system in Wales](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6500329/), *BMJ Open* 2019.
- Madden et al., evaluation of soft opt-out in Wales, *Anaesthesia* 2020.
- McLaughlin et al., evaluation of opt-out in England, *Transplantation* 2025.
- Matesanz and Domínguez-Gil, on the Spanish model, *Kidney International* 2019.
