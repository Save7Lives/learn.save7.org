- **Death is the irreversible loss of the capacity for consciousness and of the capacity to breathe.** South Africa recognises two ways of determining it: by neurological criteria (brain death) and by circulatory criteria (circulatory death).

- **Brain death is not a coma.** People recover from comas. There is no documented case of anyone who met the preconditions and criteria for brain death regaining any brain function. The heart can keep beating after brain death only because a ventilator is supplying oxygen.

- **Nothing is tested until the look-alikes are ruled out.** There must be a known, irreversible cause, a normal temperature, adequate blood pressure, no sedative effect and no severe chemical imbalance. That is the answer to "how do you know they aren't just sedated, or cold?"

- **The brain-death test has three steps:** coma, then no brainstem reflexes (checked one pathway at a time), then the apnoea test, which shows the body never tries to breathe.

- **Circulatory death needs five continuous minutes** without circulation or breathing, then no pupil response and no movement to pressure. If anything returns during the five minutes, the count restarts. The wait exists because a stopped heart can, very rarely, restart on its own in the first minutes.

- **The doctors who determine death have nothing to do with the transplant.** At least two of them where organs are to be donated, at least one with five or more years' experience.

- **Referral should happen early, before the family is approached, and it is not a legal duty.** A donation can be lost simply because nobody made the call.

- **DBD and DCD differ at almost every step.** In DCD, donation is considered only after an independent decision to withdraw treatment, nothing is done to hasten death, and if the heart does not stop in time, donation stands down and palliative care continues.

- **Tissue is not organs.** Tissue can be donated irrespective of the manner of death, hours or days later, without a ventilator, and it is stored by tissue banks. That is why far more people can donate tissue.
