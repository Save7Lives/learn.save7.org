**The guideline this Stage rests on.**
Thomson D, et al., [South African guidelines on the determination of death](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10193841/), *Southern African Journal of Critical Care* 2021;37(1b):41-54. Peer-reviewed and written for South African hospitals. It is clinical guidance for doctors, so expect detail well beyond this Stage, but its introduction and its short section on families are readable by anyone.

**The coordinators' own reference.**
The SATCS *Organ and Tissue Donation Reference File*, the Red File, sets out referral, consent, donor management and tissue donation as South African coordinators practise them. It is a professional document. Where its bedside summary differs from the guideline above (it adds an atropine test), the guideline is the authority.

**How the system is organised.**
The Southern African Transplantation Society's *Organ and Tissue Donation in South Africa: Creating a National Strategy Roadmap*, the report of a national workshop, maps the transplant centres, tissue banks and coordinator roles, and is frank about where the system falls short.

**Register, and read the answers from the source.**
The [Organ Donor Foundation of South Africa](https://odf.org.za/). Its FAQ pages answer many of the questions people ask about tissue donation.

**What not to reach for.** *7 Lives in 7 Steps* is an ICU referral algorithm for hospital staff. It is not general-public material, and nothing in this Stage depends on it.
