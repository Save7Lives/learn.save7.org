**The Act.**
The [National Health Act 61 of 2003](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a61-03.pdf), as gazetted. Chapter 8 runs from section 53 to section 68. Legislation is amended, so for anything load-bearing, check a current consolidated text as well.

**The 2012 regulations.**
Government Notice R180, *Regulations regarding the general control of human bodies, tissue, blood, blood products and gametes*, Government Gazette 35099, 2 March 2012. Regulation 9 is five lines long and worth reading in full.

**The draft regulations.**
Department of Health, [draft Regulations on Organ Transplantation](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202609/55299gon7879.pdf), Government Notice 7879, 4 September 2026. A draft for comment, not law. The document is a scan, so it cannot be searched.

**What doctors are told.**
McQuoid-Mason D, "Human tissue and organ transplant provisions: Chapter 8 of the National Health Act and its Regulations, in effect from March 2012 – what doctors must know", *South African Medical Journal* 2012;102(9):733-735. A short, readable legal summary written for clinicians.

**On donation after circulatory death.**
"Organ donation after circulatory death – legal in South Africa and in alignment with Chapter 8 of the National Health Act and Regulations relating to organ and tissue donation", *South African Journal of Bioethics and Law* 2024;17(1).
